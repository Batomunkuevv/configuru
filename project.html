3D Visualization and Virtual Tour of a Prestigious Luxury Project in New York

Our team has crafted a cutting-edge 3D tour for an iconic luxury development in New York, celebrated as one of the most slender skyscrapers in the world. This architectural marvel redefines modern elegance with its breathtaking design and commanding presence on the city skyline.

The virtual tour we developed offers an immersive experience, showcasing each residence's unique and panoramic views of New York City through its floor-to-ceiling windows. Additionally, we’ve integrated an AI-powered designer tool that allows users to effortlessly transform interiors with a single click, offering a personalized visualization of each space.

The project is further enhanced by our AI assistant, which guides prospective buyers through the virtual experience, answers queries, and gathers leads seamlessly. Together, these features combine to present an unparalleled digital representation of luxury living in the heart of Manhattan.
